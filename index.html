Privacy Policy

Kazi Pin Scheduler · Last updated October 9, 2026

What this tool is

Kazi Pin Scheduler is a personal tool used by one person, its owner. It creates Pins on the owner's own Pinterest business account to promote the owner's original designs sold on TeePublic. It is not offered to the public and has no other users.

Information it handles

The tool uses Pinterest's OAuth to act on the owner's own account only. It reads the owner's own board list and creates Pins with a title, description, image and link. It does not collect, read or store information about any other Pinterest user.

How credentials are protected

Access tokens are stored as encrypted secrets in the owner's private settings on GitHub. They are never written to logs or published.

Sharing and selling

The tool does not sell, share, analyze or advertise using any data. No data is sent to anyone other than Pinterest, to create the owner's own Pins.

Retention and deletion

The tool keeps a simple log of the Pins it created for the owner. The owner can revoke the app's access at any time in Pinterest account settings, which stops the tool and invalidates its tokens.
